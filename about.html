About Rapid Rescue Service

Who we are

Helping motorists with practical recovery support

Rapid Rescue Service provides vehicle towing, breakdown assistance, accident vehicle recovery, and vehicle transportation services for motorists in Colombo, Maharagama, Nugegoda, Dehiwala, Kottawa, and surrounding areas.

We aim to make vehicle recovery easier for our customers by providing a convenient way to request assistance and arrange transportation when unexpected vehicle problems occur.

Whether your car has broken down, your vehicle needs recovery following an accident, or you need to arrange vehicle transportation, contact us to discuss your requirements.
